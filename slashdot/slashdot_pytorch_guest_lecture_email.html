Why tensors matter

Tensor shapes, memory layouts, device placement, and operation patterns all shape the work your program actually does. Tie that mental model to a real profiler trace, and you optimize with evidence—not guesswork.

The rule of thumb: find the bottleneck first, then make measured changes. One well-chosen fix can turn a long wait into a short run—the speedup just depends on your workload and hardware.
