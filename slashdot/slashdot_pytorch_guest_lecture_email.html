Why tensors matter

Tensor shapes, memory layouts, device placement, and operation patterns all influence the work your program performs. Connecting that mental model to real profiler traces helps you optimize with evidence—not guesswork.

The goal: find the bottleneck first, then make measured improvements. A well-chosen optimization can turn a long wait into a much shorter run—but the actual speedup depends on the workload and hardware.
